How to play
  • Drag the battlefield to pan, pinch to zoom.
  • Tap one of your units to select it. Double-tap to grab every unit of that type on screen. All units selects your whole army.
  • Hold a finger still on the battlefield for a moment, then drag a box to select a group. With a group selected, tap your units to add or take them out.
  • Tap ＋ Squad (under the unit orders) to save the selected units as a squad. Tap a squad's button to select it, and again to jump to it. Hold a squad button to save the current selection over it.
  • With units selected, tap the ground to move or tap an enemy to attack.
  • Tap a Barracks, Workshop or Airbase to train units.
  • The Build menu shows when nothing is selected (tap ✕ to clear a selection). Pick a structure, tap where it goes, then tap again (or Place) to build. Green means valid.
  • Bridges extend sideways off platforms. Stack ramps into stairs that units climb automatically.
  • Music, sound effects and Preview features (new things still being tried out, such as the Isometric view) are in ⚙ Settings.
  • Battle: destroy every enemy HQ to win. Capture the Flag: HQs can't be destroyed. Walk any unit into an enemy flag to grab it, then bring it back to your HQ. Kill the carrier and the flag drops; pick up your own dropped flag and that unit takes it home. In multiplayer, a captured flag knocks that player out; the last one left wins. Flags pulse on the map.
  • Click one of your units to select it, drag a box to select a group, shift-click (or ctrl-click) to add or remove one. Double-click grabs every unit of that type on screen. Q selects your whole army.
  • Squads: Shift+F1–F4 (or ＋ Squad) saves the selected units as a squad. F1–F4 or a click on its button selects it; press again to jump to it. Right-click a squad button to save over it.
  • Right-click the ground to move, an enemy to attack, or a damaged building or vehicle to repair (engineers). Left-click works for orders too.
  • WASD or the arrow keys pan, as does dragging with the right or middle button. The wheel zooms. Space jumps back to your HQ.
  • Pick a structure with 1–8 or a click, point at the battlefield and click to build. Green means valid; right-click or Esc cancels.
  • Select a Barracks, Workshop or Airbase and press 1–4 to train. With units selected: H hold, C charge, R retreat, F find. X twice sells a building.
  • Esc clears your selection, then pauses. P pauses. M turns the music on or off.
  • Music, sound effects and Preview features are in ⚙ Settings. Turn on Preview features to try the Isometric view, set when the game starts. Isometric battles are fought on a square field with a new landscape every game: hills, a river (slow to wade, so use the bridges), roads (quick) and forests (a little slow). Send units anywhere across it. In multiplayer, the host's choice is used.
  • Battle: destroy every enemy HQ to win. Capture the Flag: HQs can't be destroyed. Walk any unit into an enemy flag to grab it, then bring it back to your HQ (C charges the nearest enemy flag). Kill the carrier and the flag drops; pick up your own dropped flag and that unit takes it home. In multiplayer, a captured flag knocks that player out; the last one left wins. Flags pulse on the map.
